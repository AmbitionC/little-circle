The orange tiger cub [[tigo]] pulls hard on the orange striped tail stuck under the sleeping bear [[bruno]]. The bear gives one huge snore with a big round puff of air from his mouth, and the tiger cub is blown backwards and tumbles head over heels softly on the grass, dizzy, not hurt. The cream tiger cub [[mimi]] holds her round ears in the wind. Everyone's mouths are soft and round, no teeth. Gentle camera.
