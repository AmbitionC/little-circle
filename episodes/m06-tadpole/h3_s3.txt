Locked-off camera, underwater. The [[goldfish]] stays where she is, always keeping a clear gap of open water between her mouth and the tiny [[tadpole]]; she never touches him and never kisses. She blinks her huge googly eyes and blows a few round bubbles into the gap. While the bubbles float up, two tiny back legs slowly grow out from the back of the tadpole's round black body, and he looks back at them in happy surprise.
