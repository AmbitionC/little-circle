[[bruno]] wearing a too-small headband with two big round pink mouse ears and a long pink yarn tail tied around his waist bursts out of a big round bush with his arms up in a playful 'boo' pose and a goofy grin, holding a big pumpkin basket full of colorful wrapped candies. Then he laughs so hard his belly wobbles, the headband slides down over one eye and the yarn tail falls off. Moonlit night, glowing pumpkin lanterns. Playful and friendly, not scary; readable motion for toddlers.
