Seen from behind the whole time: [[nutty]], [[hazel]] and [[bun]] in their costumes keep slowly walking away from the camera down the moonlit path, their backs to the camera, they never turn around and never look back. Wrapped candies drop one by one from the hole in the bottom of the squirrel's pouch onto the path. Behind the big tree on the left, the huge soft round shadow of a giant cartoon mouse with two big round ears slowly slides a little closer and bends to pick up one fallen candy. In the last second the hedgehog's spines pop out through his ghost sheet and the bunny's ears flop down, still facing away. The shadow stays round, blobby and cute, no eyes, no teeth, never scary.
