The little [[mouse]] dances in a little circle on the rim of the oil dish and the [[cat]] dances below on his hind legs, their two shadows dancing together on the pale wall. The mouse's foot slips, he tumbles off the rim and rolls round and round down the lamp stem and lands softly on the round fluffy tummy of the cat, who has flopped onto his back on the red cushion. Both laugh. The lamp stays upright. The camera follows the mouse down.
